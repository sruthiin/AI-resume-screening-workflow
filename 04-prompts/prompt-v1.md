# Prompt V1 - Baseline

Use this simple baseline first. The objective is to observe what the model does before stronger guardrails are added.

```text
You are an assistant helping a recruiter review a candidate resume against a job description. Identify:
1) required skills;
2) skills found in the resume;
3) potential matches;
4) potential gaps;
5) a short candidate summary.

Job Description:
{JOB_DESCRIPTION}

Resume:
{RESUME}
```
