# Project Objective

Design and evaluate an AI-assisted resume screening workflow that extracts evidence from synthetic resumes, compares that evidence with job requirements, identifies matches and gaps, and produces an explainable summary for human review.

## Primary Objective
Demonstrate how GenAI can support recruitment analysis while preserving human authority and responsible-AI controls.

## Secondary Objectives
- Translate a recruitment business problem into functional and AI-quality requirements.
- Design an AS-IS and TO-BE workflow.
- Develop and compare three prompt versions.
- Create synthetic data representing realistic matching and edge cases.
- Test extraction, matching, uncertainty, hallucination, fairness, consistency, and decision-boundary behavior.
- Log meaningful defects and define risk controls.
- Produce a recruiter-friendly GitHub evidence trail.

## Out of Scope
Autonomous rejection, AI-only final hiring decisions, real candidate data, production ATS integration, employment verification, and legal/compliance certification.
