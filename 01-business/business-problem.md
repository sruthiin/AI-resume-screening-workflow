# Business Problem

## Fictional Company
**TalentFlow** - fictional recruitment operations organization.

## Problem Statement
TalentFlow receives a high volume of resumes. Recruiters spend significant time manually extracting candidate skills and experience, comparing those details with job requirements, and preparing summaries for review.

The proposed portfolio prototype uses a large language model to organize candidate evidence before recruiter review. The workflow must reduce repetitive analysis effort without allowing the AI to make the final hiring decision.

## Business Need
The workflow should help a recruiter quickly answer:

1. What requirements does the job description state?
2. What relevant evidence is explicitly present in the resume?
3. Which requirements are supported, partially supported, unsupported, or unknown?
4. What evidence or context should the recruiter verify?

## Business Risks
The workflow is exposed to hallucination, false-positive matches, false-negative matches, bias from irrelevant personal attributes, privacy exposure, overconfidence, and automation of a decision that should remain human-owned.

## Success Definition
A successful portfolio prototype demonstrates a traceable chain from requirements to workflow, prompts, actual tests, evidence-backed findings, responsible-AI controls, and human review. It does **not** claim production accuracy, legal compliance, or real hiring outcomes.
