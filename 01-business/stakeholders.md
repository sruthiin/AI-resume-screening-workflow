# Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Primary Concern | What This Project Demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| Recruiter | Efficiency and accurate evidence | Structured, reviewable candidate analysis |
| Hiring Manager | Relevant experience and gaps | Requirement-to-evidence mapping |
| Candidate | Fair and accurate representation | Evidence grounding and irrelevant-attribute exclusion |
| HR / Talent Operations | Consistency and process efficiency | Repeatable workflow and testing |
| Business Analyst | Requirements and workflow clarity | Traceability from problem to controls |
| AI / Engineering | Implementable model behavior | Explicit input/output and guardrails |
| AI Quality / QA | Testing and evaluation | Test strategy, defects, retesting |
| Risk / Responsible AI | Privacy, fairness, explainability, oversight | Paired tests, uncertainty, human checkpoint |
